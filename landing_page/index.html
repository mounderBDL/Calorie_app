How it works

From photo to nutrition
in 3 simple steps

No manual data entry needed. Just point, adjust, and save.

1
Scan your meal

Take a photo or upload from your gallery. The AI model analyzes it on-device and returns the top food match with a confidence score.

2
Adjust the portions

Review ingredients and fine-tune gram amounts with the +/− stepper. Add extras or remove what isn't on your plate.

3
Save & track

Save the meal. Your home screen updates instantly — calorie ring, macro bars, and daily progress all reflect the new meal.

FAQ

Questions, answered honestly.

Is there really a free version?
Yes, completely free with no time limit. You get 3 scans per day, 7-day meal history, manual entry, and full ingredient adjustment. No credit card, no trial. Just download and use.
Why should I pay? The free version seems generous.
You probably don't need to. The free tier handles most daily use cases. Premium is for people who want unlimited scans, full history beyond 7 days, and PDF export for dietitians or serious tracking.
Can I try Premium before paying?
We're planning a 14-day free trial for Premium. For now, the free tier gives you a solid feel for the app before committing.
Do you have a family plan?
Coming in Phase 2 — 5 accounts for 1,500 DZD/year. That's 300 DZD per person per year. If you're interested, contact us and we'll notify you when it launches.
